Why we built this

A 500g block of Meiji butter can cost $8.90 at NTUC and $6.95 at Sheng Siong on the same day. A tray of eggs may be $3.80 at Cold Storage and $2.60 at NTUC. These aren't sales — this is just how differently supermarkets price the same products.

Most Singaporeans shop at whichever supermarket is closest, never knowing they're consistently paying 10–30% more for specific items. Cartly makes the comparison instant — type an item name and see every price, ranked, in under a minute.

The basket builder takes it further: add your weekly groceries, and Cartly calculates the cheapest way to split your shopping across stores.

How it works

1

You search for an item

Type any grocery item — a brand, a product, or a category. Cartly searches all five stores simultaneously in real time.

2

We scrape the live search results

Each store's search results page is loaded in a headless browser (or Algolia API for Giant). We extract names, prices, promotions, and images — then cache results for 6 hours so repeat searches are instant.

3

Results are matched and ranked

Products from different stores are clustered by similarity using IDF-weighted name matching and unit parsing. The same product across stores appears in one card, sorted cheapest first.

4

Build your basket

Add items to your basket as you search. The order summary shows your total, how much you'd save versus worst-case prices, and the optimal store split.
