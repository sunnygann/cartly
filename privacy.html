Privacy Policy

We collect as little as possible and never sell your data. Here's exactly what we do and don't do.

📥 1. What we collect

Short version: we store your search queries (to cache results) and your email address only if you sign up for price alerts.
DataWhenWhy
Search query text Every search Cached in our database so repeat searches within 6 hours return instantly without re-scraping
Store filter preference When you toggle a store chip Saved in your browser's localStorage only — never sent to our servers
Email address Only if you submit the price alert form To notify you when a product's price drops below your target. The form is currently UI-only and not yet active.

🚫 2. What we don't collect

  • No account registration — you never create a profile
  • No cookies (we use localStorage for store preferences, which stays on your device)
  • No IP address logging beyond what Railway's infrastructure records by default
  • No device fingerprinting or behavioural tracking
  • No third-party analytics (no Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.)
  • No advertising identifiers

⚙️ 3. How we use it

Search queries are stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Railway. They are used solely to:

  • Cache price results for 6 hours so repeat searches are fast
  • Determine whether a fresh scrape is needed

We do not analyse search queries for advertising, do not share them with third parties, and do not use them to build user profiles.

If you provide your email address for price alerts, it will only be used to send the specific alerts you requested. You can unsubscribe at any time by emailing us.

🗄️ 4. Where data is stored

All server-side data (search query cache, price history, store data) is stored in a PostgreSQL database on Railway, hosted in their Singapore region. Data does not leave Singapore infrastructure.

Store filter preferences are stored entirely in your browser's localStorage under the key cartly_stores. We never receive this data.

🕐 5. Retention

DataRetention period
Scraped query cache 6 hours (records older than 6h are treated as expired and overwritten on next search)
Price history records 30 days (used for the price history chart; older records may be purged)
Email addresses (price alerts) Until you unsubscribe or request deletion
localStorage (store preferences) Until you clear your browser data — entirely under your control

🔗 6. Third-party services

Cartly uses the following third-party services. We have no control over their privacy practices.

ServicePurposeData shared
Railway Backend hosting and database All server-side data is stored here. Railway Privacy Policy
Surge Frontend hosting (cartly.surge.sh) Surge may log request IPs for CDN routing. Surge Privacy Policy
Cloudflare (1.1.1.1) DNS-over-HTTPS for Giant scraper Domain lookups only (no user data). Cloudflare Privacy Policy

Price data is scraped from the public search pages of NTUC FairPrice, Cold Storage, Sheng Siong, Lazada (RedMart & Donki), and Giant. Cartly is not affiliated with any of these retailers.

🛡️ 7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of any personal data we hold about you
  • Deletion — request that we delete your email address or any other personal data
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate personal data
  • Objection — opt out of any use of your data

Given the minimal data we collect, most users have nothing stored beyond anonymous search query cache entries, which expire automatically within 6 hours. Email us to exercise any of these rights.
